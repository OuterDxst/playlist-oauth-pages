# Privacy notice

**App:** BIBIBAR Playlist OAuth  
**Contact:** [morgesoburee@gmail.com](mailto:morgesoburee@gmail.com)  
**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

The app uses Google sign-in to verify the BIBIBAR account email and YouTube
channel. It requests YouTube access to read playlist information and create,
update, or verify the owner's playlists. The owner supplies playlist requests;
the app stores request and result records locally. Completed playlist links and
results may also be saved to the owner's Google Drive and sent through the
owner's existing Telegram notification workflow.

Google OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in Windows protected local storage
on the owner's computer. They are not included in public source files or in
playlist requests. Google and YouTube process data under their own policies.

The local automation does not sell Google user data or offer third-party account
access. The owner can revoke access at
[Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) and can
request deletion of the locally held OAuth credentials and playlist records at
the contact address above. Revocation stops future API access but does not undo
playlist changes already made in YouTube.
