# Privacy notice

**App:** James Playlist OAuth  
**Contact:** [james1.bae@gmail.com](mailto:james1.bae@gmail.com)  
**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

The app uses Google sign-in to verify the owner's account email and YouTube
channel. It requests YouTube access to read playlist information and create or
verify playlists from the owner's requests. The requests and results, including
song lists and playlist links, are stored locally. The owner's existing
workflow may also save requests and results in the owner's Google Drive and
send result notifications through Telegram.

Google OAuth credentials and tokens are kept in Windows protected local storage
on the owner's computer. They are not included in public source files or
playlist requests. Google and YouTube process data under their own policies.

The local automation does not sell Google user data or offer third-party account
access. The owner can revoke access at
[Google Account connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) and can
request deletion of locally held OAuth credentials and playlist records at the
contact address above. Revocation stops future API access but does not undo
playlists already created in YouTube.
