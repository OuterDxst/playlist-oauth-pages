# BIBIBAR Playlist OAuth

BIBIBAR Playlist OAuth is an owner-operated desktop automation for the BIBIBAR
YouTube channel. It reads the channel's playlists and updates the BIBIBAR Lunch
and Dinner playlists from requests prepared by the owner. It does not offer a
public sign-up or ask visitors to connect their Google accounts.

The app requests your Google account email and YouTube account access. YouTube
access lets the app read your channel and playlists and create or change
playlists. Grant access only if you are the BIBIBAR account owner and intend to
run this local automation.
